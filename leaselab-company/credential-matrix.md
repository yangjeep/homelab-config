## Enforced authority, not broad tokens

GitHub does not have a native merge-only permission. `contents:write` can also permit merges; it must not be described as inherently Engineer-can-push/cannot-merge. Keep the seven role Apps distinct. Existing role Apps can back trusted controllers only when elevated grants stay service-side and normal role tokens retain fixed reduced scopes; those tokens are not separate GitHub identities from their parent App. Give CoS, Support, QA, SRE and Growth no Contents-write credential. Engineer needs actor-restricted main/prod rules and ref constraints or a ref-allowlisted Git service. Reviewer and SRE use narrow authenticated service clients, with merge/release credentials kept solely in the corresponding service.

Main actor restrictions and no-bypass quality requirements are separate: only the Reviewer merge App may satisfy the actor rule through PR-only bypass, while it cannot bypass CI, current SHA QA and independent review. Trusted App-bound checks and immutable evidence must reject missing/stale/ambiguous approvals. A PR label or arbitrary JSON submission is not a QA authorization boundary. Main and prod direct writes, privileged workflows and other mutation routes must be denied to unauthorized actors. Broader provider tokens, GitHub Actions-write and arbitrary cloud SQL capabilities stay service-side with allowlisted operations. Founder emergency authority stays outside autonomous execution.

Before enabling each credential, record its provider identity, nonsecret scope/resource identifiers, last-verified date, safe positive/negative test evidence and rotation owner in the deployment validation record. Verify Google property discovery and read-only report/query; prove administrative denial without making production mutations. Verify non-SRE actors cannot reach migration/promotion credentials and that the SRE client permits only approved artifacts. Unavailable OAuth/App grants, provider limitations or unfinished enforcement remain BLOCKED; no substitute founder token or unrestricted key is acceptable.
